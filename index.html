Controls

Pick something from the catalog, click to place it, then grab, throw, shoot and blow things up. Dummies feel pain, bleed, break bones, lose consciousness and try to get back on their feet.

Grab & throwLeft-drag
Use held item (fire, toggle, pull pin)Space / F
Use item under cursorRight-click
Rotate held itemQ E / wheel
Pan cameraRight-drag, WASD
ZoomWheel / pinch
Flip before placingF
Place severalShift + click
Inspect a dummyRight-click it
Delete under cursorX / Del
Tools1–7
Pause / slow motionP / T
Health overlayL
Clear blood & debrisC
Automatic weapons fire while Space or the right button is held.
